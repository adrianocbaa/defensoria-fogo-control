# Avaliações — shared modules

- Criterion 1 relationship charts come from the pure `relacionamento.ts` over the stored execution coefficients and `regression_input` (vary one variable within its observed range, others at their original-scale mean, inverse of the dependent transform); the evaluator's visual analysis is recorded only through `aval_relationship_visual_review` in `audit_events`, and becomes a manual review only when the policy rule is not AUTOMATICO. Why: diagnostics never refit the model nor invent normative obligations.
- Criterion 12 (Outliers) is classified by `classifyOutliers` from the policy's OUTLIERS thresholds (decision metric named explicitly, other diagnostics shown as complementary; empty thresholds keep the legacy always-review behavior); it never fails a model, and per-sample evaluator decisions go only through `aval_outlier_decision` into `audit_events`, with exclusion creating a derived model via the existing model creation flow. Why: suspicious points may be legitimate market data and the validated model must never change silently.
